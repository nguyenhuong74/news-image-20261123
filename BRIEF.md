## The six stories in the image

### 1. COP31 ENDS WITH NO DEAL — climate, global
- The UN COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, ended (Sunday 22 Nov,
  closing plenary) without an overarching agreement — the first COP to break
  down with no final decision since Copenhagen 2009, and the longest COP on
  record (two days of overtime).
- Deadlock: the final "Antalya Pact" draft dropped any mention of fossil
  fuels; the EU and climate-vulnerable states refused to endorse it.
- Reported across the wires on Monday 23 Nov.
- Sources: World Resources Institute statement (Antalya, 22 Nov 2026);
  BusinessWorld/Reuters, "COP31 climate talks collapse as EU and others
  reject deal", 23 Nov 2026; Oceanographic Magazine, 23 Nov 2026.

### 2. UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN WARSHIP OFF LIBYA — war, Mediterranean
- President Zelenskyy confirmed on Monday 23 Nov that Ukrainian naval
  drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base in
  Libya (attack on Sunday 22 Nov) and hit the cargo ship MV Angara.
- Seven Ukrainian-made naval drones; a Ka-27 helicopter aboard was also
  destroyed. The Merkury was commissioned in 2023. Ukraine's first
  officially acknowledged strike in Libya — the war reaching the
  Mediterranean, ~2,400 km from the front line.
- Sources: Reuters via Military Times, 23 Nov 2026 10:17 UTC; Associated
  Press, 23 Nov 2026; Reuters, "Zelenskiy says Ukraine sank Russian
  corvette near Libya's coast", 23 Nov 2026.

### 3. 15 KILLED IN STRIKES AROUND KYIV — war, Ukraine
- Overnight Russian strikes on the Kyiv region killed at least 15 people,
  reported Monday 23 Nov: 11 in a five-storey block hit in Dymer (45 km
  north of Kyiv), 3 in a supermarket strike in Vyshneve, 1 in Bila
  Tserkva, plus 1 in Zaporizhzhia. A 7-year-old boy was pulled alive from
  the rubble in Dymer after 14 hours.
- Zelenskyy pressed the US to release Patriot interceptors already paid
  for by partners.
- Sources: BBC/Al Jazeera via EuropeSays, 23 Nov 2026 08:20 UTC; Kyiv
  Post, 23 Nov 2026; Al Jazeera, 23 Nov 2026 07:32 UTC.

### 4. 24 DEAD IN INDONESIA FACTORY FIRE — disaster, Southeast Asia
- Indonesia confirmed on Monday 23 Nov that 24 people died in the fire
  that tore through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory in
  Cikande, Banten (fire broke out Saturday evening 21 Nov), with about
  30 people still missing and rescuers searching unstable wreckage.
- Preliminary reports point to an air-conditioner short circuit; the
  factory makes the Milkita candy brand.
- Sources: Reuters, "Indonesia says 24 killed in factory blaze as
  rescuers search for dozens missing", 23 Nov 2026; BusinessWorld, 23 Nov
  2026 19:06; Wikipedia, "2026 Serang Regency factory fire".

### 5. BEPICOLOMBO SAFE IN MERCURY ORBIT — space, Europe/Japan
- ESA said on Monday 23 Nov that the European-Japanese BepiColombo
  spacecraft is safe and in a stable orbit around Mercury after its
  eight-year journey — orbit insertion on 21 Nov, when a software glitch
  cut the thruster burn short and the craft entered safe mode.
- The glitch was traced to a software issue; the mission stays on track
  for its two orbiters to separate on 9 December.
- Sources: ESA, "BepiColombo safely in Mercury orbit despite unexpected
  challenge", 23 Nov 2026; Reuters, "Mercury spacecraft BepiColombo 'on
  track' despite glitch, ESA says", 23 Nov 2026; Gizmodo, 23 Nov 2026.

### 6. RIEMANN PROOF: LEAN CHECKS ADVANCE — science/tech
- The claimed AI-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis (Anthropic
  mathematician Levent Alpöge, working with Claude; announced 20 Nov as
  ~17 million lines of Lean code) kept moving on 23 Nov: independent
  machine verification advanced (Lean kernel stages accepted during 23
  Nov UTC), the Lean/Mathlib community stood by its confidence, and
  Alpöge clarified on 23 Nov that the "250,000-page proof" figure was a
  joke. Experts remain divided; the Clay Institute still lists the
  problem unsolved and no human yet understands the full proof.
- Sources: Phys.org, "'The sacred object in mathematics': The Riemann
  hypothesis, AI and a proof no human can read", 23 Nov 2026; TradingView/
  Invezz, 23 Nov 2026 13:26 UTC; explainx.ai verification tracker, updates
  of 23 Nov 2026; Wikipedia, "Alpöge proof of the Riemann hypothesis".
